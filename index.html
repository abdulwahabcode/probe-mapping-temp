Our Bakes

From the oven, with amore

Every cake is baked to order — never frozen, never boxed mix. Tell us your occasion and we'll bake for it.

Wedding Cakes

torte nuziali

Multi-tier wedding cakes designed around your theme — classic vanilla bean, lemon-ricotta, chocolate-hazelnut, and more, finished with fresh flowers or hand piping.

Custom Celebration Cakes

torte per feste

Birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, baptisms — any size, any theme, decorated by hand to match your celebration.

Italian Pastries

pasticceria

Cannoli with sweet ricotta, flaky sfogliatelle, cream-filled zeppole, and seasonal specialties — sold by the box or the tray.
