What we do

Services built for working businesses

Straightforward accounting and compliance services for contractors, shops, restaurants and service businesses across the Las Vegas Valley.

01

Tax Preparation

Individual and small-business tax returns prepared accurately and filed on time — federal and Nevada. We look for every deduction you're entitled to, and explain everything in plain language.

02

Bookkeeping & Accounting

Monthly books, profit-and-loss statements and organized records that keep your business audit-ready. Know exactly where your money goes, every month.

03

Contractor License Assistance

Step-by-step help with Nevada contractor license applications, renewals and paperwork. We know the forms, the timelines and the common mistakes that cause delays.

Payroll Services

Employee payroll, tax withholdings and year-end filings handled correctly and on schedule — so you can focus on the job site, not the paperwork.

LLC setup, EIN applications and business licenses — everything you need to start operating legally in Nevada, handled in the right order.
