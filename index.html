Services

Care that fits your dog's routine

Every visit is built around what your pup already knows and loves — same schedule, same cuddle spots, same treats.

In-Home Pet Sitting

Your pup stays in their own home while you're away — familiar beds, familiar routines, zero kennel stress. Ideal for vacations and long trips.

Drop-In Visits

Quick daytime check-ins for potty breaks, fresh water, playtime and meds — perfect for workdays, errands and dinner plans.

Dog Walking

Neighborhood walks sized for small breeds — gentle pace, leash-safe routes, shade breaks in the Arizona heat and plenty of sniff stops.

Overnight Stays

A sitter stays overnight in your home so your dog's evening routine never changes — dinner, last potty break and bedtime cuddles included.

Puppy Care

Extra-attention visits for puppies — potty training support, crate routines, feeding schedules and socialization play sessions.

Medication & Special Needs

Timed medications, special diets and senior-dog care handled with patience — plus written notes after every visit so you always know how it went.
